### 📌 Executive Summary
I build production-grade Generative AI agents, voice copilots, and search intelligence tooling. My work combines full-stack AI engineering (FastAPI, Whisper, SQLite WAL) with Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and data-backed product strategy to drive measurable business outcomes.

- 🔭 **Active Engineering:** Architecting [likhlo-ai](https://github.com/aiproductivity-akku/likhlo-ai) (low-latency voice-to-ledger agent for Indian micro-merchants) and [LuciferAI](https://github.com/aiproductivity-akku/LuciferAI) (Generative Engine Optimization & Citability Studio).
- 🧠 **Domain Expertise:** Speech-to-Intent inference, structured LLM extraction (Pydantic/Instructor), Agentic workflows, and GEO citability auditing for AI search engines.
- 📈 **Growth & Product:** Designing end-to-end user journeys, viral loops, and high-retention onboarding architectures.
